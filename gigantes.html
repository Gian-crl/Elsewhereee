Isla Gigantes

📍 Carles, Iloilo, Philippines

About the Place

Isla Gigantes is a group of islands in Carles, Iloilo, known for its beautiful beaches, clear waters, rock formations, and scenic island landscapes. It is a popular destination for visitors who enjoy island adventures and relaxing by the sea.

Travel Tips
